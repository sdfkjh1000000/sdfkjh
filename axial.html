The axial grid

One dominant axis instead of a uniform grid. A single vertical rule is the spine; everything hangs off it. Rigid structure, editorial result.

Study page. Noindex, unlinked. After the Musashino "Chairs for All" poster.

Six moves

  1. One dominant axis.A single off-centre rule; everything aligns to it.
  2. Hung content.Material to one side, the empty side deliberate.
  3. Horizontal banding.Sliced into stacked bands, hairline rules between.
  4. The object, exploded.The subject cut into material layers. The focal point.
  5. Vertical text on the axis.A title set vertically, riding the spine.
  6. Markers and colophon.Numbers hung to the axis, dense info low.
