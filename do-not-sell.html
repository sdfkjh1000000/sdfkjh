Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Your rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

We don't sell or share your data

sdfkjh.com does not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140(ad)). We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by the CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140(ah)).

We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.

What we collect

This site does not collect personal information. There is no contact form, no user accounts, and no analytics. The only way to contact us is via a direct email link — no data is transmitted to us through this website. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. No external fonts, scripts, analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are loaded on its own — fonts and images are served from this domain. The on-page command console can optionally call public third-party APIs (wttr.in, Hacker News, NVD) when you run specific commands, and only then; see the privacy policy for details. Neither Cloudflare hosting nor those on-demand lookups constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under CCPA/CPRA.

Your rights under CCPA/CPRA

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected and how it is used
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (not applicable — we don't sell or share)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise your rights or ask questions about our data practices: info@sdfkjh.com
